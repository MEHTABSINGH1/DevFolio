About me

Curious builder, always learning.

I'm a first-year B.Tech student at Kalvium learning to build for the web — one project at a time.

AM

My story

I started with zero coding experience and a lot of curiosity. My first webpage was a plain HTML page — but the moment it rendered in the browser, I was hooked.

Since then I've learned to structure pages with semantic HTML, style them with modern CSS layouts, and bring them to life with JavaScript. I believe the best way to learn is to build real things and ship them.

When I'm not coding, you'll find me sketching UI ideas, reading about design, or helping classmates debug their layouts.
